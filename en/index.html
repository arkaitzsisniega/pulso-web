What PULSO is

Two tools built for the coaching staff of a futsal team. They are sold separately and, together, they talk to each other: what you log on the timer goes to the web app.

The match timer

On the iPad on the bench. You log the match and the report is ready.

  • Minutes and rotations for each player
  • Each goal with its play and the five on court
  • PDF report at the final whistle

How it works

Three steps. You tell us about your club and we get everything ready.

  1. Club sign-up

    Send us the basics: crest, colours, squad, competitions and who will use it. Choose the timer, the web app or both.

  2. We set it up

    We configure your timer and your web app, load your squad and train whoever is going to use it.

  3. Your club uses it

    From the first match and the first session. Any question or change, just tell us and we adjust it.

We look after the data

We handle the club's data in line with the GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation). Each team has its own password and only sees its own data.
